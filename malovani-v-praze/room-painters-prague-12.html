Room painters Prague 12

Malování v Praze offers its services throughout Prague and the surrounding area. That includes room painters for Prague 12. We take care of painting areas of various sizes, from the smallest rooms to large halls. Room painters Prague 12 can be a lifesaver for you if you are not one of those people who enjoy painting. Since our services are comprehensive, cleaning or moving furniture and household items will also be arranged for you.

How the painting works

  1. 01

    No-obligation quote

    Call us or get in touch. We will prepare a no-obligation quote.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    We cover the floor and everything that could be stained by paint, and prepare the walls.

  3. 03

    Painting

    We paint on schedule and to your taste. We will help you choose the colours.

  4. 04

    Clean-up

    We clean up perfectly after ourselves and put the furniture back in its original state.

Choosing colours and materials

We will help you choose colours and materials that suit your taste as well as practical requirements. We work with proven paint brands to ensure long life and a beautiful look of the walls.
